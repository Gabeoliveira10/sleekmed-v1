Why Americans Overpay for Prescriptions

The prescription drug pricing system has four systemic failures. SleekMed addresses all of them.

01

PBM Markups Are Hidden

Pharmacy Benefit Managers (PBMs) operate between insurers and pharmacies. They pocket the spread between what insurers pay and what pharmacies receive — and you're never told the size of that spread.

02

Insurance Co-Pays Aren't Always Cheapest

For generic medications, your insurance co-pay is often higher than the cash price available through discount networks. Pharmacies are forbidden by "gag clauses" from telling you this without being asked.

03

Price Opacity by Design

Drug manufacturers set one "list price" but negotiate dozens of secret rebate tiers. The price you see at the counter is the price after your insurer's rebate was already collected — without passing savings to you.

04

No Single Source of Truth

GoodRx, Cost Plus, and your insurance each have different price points for the same drug. Until now, comparing all three required visiting multiple platforms. SleekMed shows all four prices in one place, instantly.

Platform Education

How SleekMed Works

A deep dive into the technology, network, and clinical logic behind our platform.

01

Our Pricing Engine

SleekMed's pricing engine aggregates data from four sources in real time: our proprietary affiliate network of 75,000+ pharmacies, the GoodRx pricing API, Mark Cuban's Cost Plus Drug Company, and national insurance carrier formulary databases. Prices are updated continuously throughout the day.

Note: Insurance co-pay estimates are based on formulary tier data and may vary by plan and deductible status. Always confirm your final cost with the pharmacy before dispensing.
02

The SleekMed Direct Price

Our "SleekMed Direct" price is the negotiated rate available through our proprietary affiliate card. When you present your Digital Access Card at a partner pharmacy, our BIN/PCN routing code is applied at the point of sale, and the negotiated price is honored immediately — no claims, no reimbursement delays, no prior authorizations.

Affiliate Disclosure: SleekMed earns a small affiliate fee per claim ($2.50–$5.00) from our partner network. This fee is never passed to the consumer. It is how we sustain a free platform.
03

Insurance Routing Logic

When you provide your insurance details in the Security Vault, SleekMed cross-references your carrier and Group ID against our formulary database to calculate an estimated co-pay for your specific plan tier. This estimate is displayed alongside cash prices in every search result. If your insurance co-pay is lower, use your insurance. If cash is lower, use SleekMed Direct. We tell you the truth — always.

04

HIPAA & Data Security

All personal and insurance data stored in your Security Vault is encrypted client-side before storage. SleekMed does not sell, share, or license your personal health information to any third party. You may permanently purge all data at any time from within the Vault.

HIPAA Commitment: SleekMed is designed to align with HIPAA data handling best practices for consumer health applications. We are a price aggregation tool, not a covered entity, but we hold ourselves to the same standard.

Why Americans Overpay for Prescriptions

01

Insurance Co-Pays Aren't Always Cheapest

For generic medications, your insurance co-pay is often higher than the cash price available through discount networks. Pharmacies are forbidden by "gag clauses" from telling you this without being asked.

02

Price Opacity by Design

Drug manufacturers set one "list price" but negotiate dozens of secret rebate tiers. The price you see at the counter is the price after your insurer's rebate was already collected.

03

No Single Source of Truth

GoodRx, Cost Plus, and your insurance each have different price points for the same drug. SleekMed shows all prices in one place, instantly.

Questions & Answers

How does SleekMed find the lowest price?

We aggregate live data from multiple discount networks, cash-pay pharmacy systems, and standard insurance formularies to show you a direct side-by-side comparison.

Do I need health insurance to use this?

No. Our platform is built to show you the best cash prices available, which are frequently lower than standard insurance co-pays anyway.

Is my personal data secure?

Yes. All data entered into your Security Vault is encrypted and stored locally. We comply with strict data handling standards and never sell your information.

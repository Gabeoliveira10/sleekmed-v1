Clinical Transparency

Why You Overpay.

The prescription pricing system has three systemic failures. Vital Rx solves all three.

Co-Pays Aren't Always Cheapest

For generic drugs, the retail cash price is often dramatically higher than what's available through direct discount networks. Pharmacists are legally barred by gag clauses from disclosing lower prices.

Hidden Rebate Tiers

Manufacturers negotiate secret rebates with your insurer. Those savings are collected at the corporate level and never passed to you at the pharmacy counter.

No Single Source of Truth

GoodRx, Cost Plus, and retail pharmacies all quote different prices for the same drug. Vital Rx shows every source simultaneously — in one place, instantly — so the lowest price is obvious.

Our Pricing Engine

Vital Rx's pricing engine aggregates data from four sources: our proprietary affiliate card network, the GoodRx pricing API, Mark Cuban's Cost Plus Drug Company, and retail cash prices. Prices are estimates and may vary at the point of sale.

The Vital Rx Partner Rate

Our "Vital Rx Partner Rate" price is the negotiated rate available through our proprietary affiliate card. When you present your Digital Card at a partner pharmacy, our BIN/PCN routing code is applied at the point of sale and the negotiated price is honored immediately.

Affiliate Disclosure: Vital Rx earns a small affiliate fee per claim ($2.50–$5.00) from our partner network. This fee is never passed to the consumer.

HIPAA & Data Security

All personal data stored in your profile is encrypted client-side before storage. Vital Rx does not sell, share, or license your personal health information to any third party. Vital Rx is designed to align with HIPAA data handling best practices for consumer health applications.

Eligibility & Legal Compliance

Federal law (42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b) prohibits government beneficiaries (Medicare, Medicaid, VA, TRICARE) from using manufacturer savings cards. Vital Rx's eligibility check ensures compliance. Cash prices visible to all users without restriction.

NOT Insurance

Vital Rx is a price aggregation and data display tool. We are NOT health insurance providers, NOT doctors, and NOT medical practitioners. Prices shown are estimates and may vary. Always consult a licensed physician before starting, changing, or stopping any medication.

Digital Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Vital Rx is committed to making our platform accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities.

Our Commitment

Vital Rx strives to ensure its services are accessible to people with disabilities. We believe every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence — and that includes accessing healthcare pricing information freely and easily.

Conformance Status

Vital Rx aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including those with visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive impairments.

Accessibility Features

  • Full keyboard navigation throughout the application
  • Screen-reader compatible — semantic HTML5 with ARIA roles and labels
  • Skip-to-content link for keyboard and screen-reader users
  • Sufficient color contrast ratios on all text and interactive elements
  • Visible focus indicators on all interactive elements
  • Reduced-motion support via prefers-reduced-motion media query
  • Descriptive alt text on all meaningful images
  • No content flashes more than three times per second
  • Responsive design that works across desktop, tablet, and mobile

Technical Specifications

Vital Rx relies on the following technologies for conformance with WCAG 2.1:

  • HTML5 with semantic landmark elements (header, main, nav, footer)
  • CSS3 with system-preference media queries
  • Vanilla JavaScript with accessible event handling and focus management

These pages are tested with modern browsers and assistive technologies including VoiceOver (macOS/iOS) and NVDA (Windows).

Known Limitations

Despite our best efforts, some limitations exist. We are actively working to resolve them:

  • Interactive price charts — SVG data visualizations may not be fully described for screen-reader users. Text-based alternatives are in progress.
  • Pharmacy location map — relies on browser geolocation APIs with varying assistive technology support across platforms.

Feedback & Contact

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of Vital Rx. If you encounter any barriers or have suggestions, please reach out — we aim to respond within 2 business days.

    How SleekMed Works
    Everything you need to know about saving on your prescriptions.
    What is SleekMed and is it free?
    SleekMed is a healthcare price intelligence platform. We negotiate directly with pharmacies to get you prices that are often lower than your insurance co pay. Yes, the basic access card is completely free to use.
    How do I use the Access Card at the pharmacy?
    Simply log in, tap on the Card tab, and show your digital screen to the pharmacist. They will enter the BIN, PCN, and GROUP codes to apply your discount immediately.
    Where is SleekMed accepted?
    Our proprietary network is accepted at over 75,000 pharmacies nationwide, including major chains like CVS, Walgreens, Walmart, Kroger, and Costco.
    Can I use this with my insurance?
    SleekMed is not insurance. You cannot combine it with your insurance or government programs. However, you can ask the pharmacist to run both and simply choose to pay whichever price is lower.

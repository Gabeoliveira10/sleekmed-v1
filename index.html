    About SleekMed
    Our mission to revolutionize healthcare pricing.

    The Problem We Are Solving

    For decades the pharmaceutical supply chain has operated in the shadows. Manufacturers sell to wholesalers who sell to pharmacies all while Pharmacy Benefit Managers negotiate secret rebates. The end result is that the patient at the counter has zero visibility into what a medication actually costs to produce and dispense.

    This lack of transparency leads to situations where paying cash is often cheaper than using insurance but patients are never told this at the register.

    The SleekMed Solution

    We built a technology engine that aggregates pricing data from thousands of endpoints across the United States. We do not manufacture drugs and we do not own pharmacies. Our sole product is truth.

    By empowering patients with clear comparative data we are forcing the industry to compete on price and efficiency rather than hidden contracts.

    What exactly is SleekMed?
    We are an unbiased healthcare price aggregator. We collect data from various discount networks direct to consumer pharmacies and standard insurers to show you all your options on one screen.
    How much does it cost to use?
    The platform and the digital access card are completely free to use. You simply pay the discounted price of the medication directly to the pharmacy.
    How do I use the digital card?
    Create a free account and navigate to the Card tab. Show that screen to your pharmacist when dropping off or paying for your medication. They will enter the routing numbers to apply your discount.
    Do I need insurance to use this?
    No insurance is required. In fact many of our users find that our negotiated cash prices are actually lower than their insurance co pays.
    Can I combine this with my insurance?
    Our discounts cannot be stacked or combined with insurance or Medicare. However you can ask your pharmacist to compare our price against your insurance price and simply select whichever is cheaper.

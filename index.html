Our Mission
The right price for your medicine shouldn't be a secret.

Vital Rx was born out of a gap in the system. While transitioning between insurance plans, I found myself at the pharmacy counter without coverage, facing an out-of-pocket bill for essential medication. When the system couldn't provide transparency on pricing, I looked elsewhere and discovered that a third-party code was significantly cheaper than the insurance I had been paying hundreds for every month.

That experience revealed a fundamental flaw in healthcare: the negotiated price isn't always the best price. I built Vital Rx to pull back the curtain, giving every patient the transparency they deserve and the tools to find the real price of their medication.

— Gabriel Oliveira, Founder & CEO

Clinical Transparency

Why You Overpay.

The prescription pricing system has three systemic failures. Vital Rx solves all three.

Co-Pays Aren't Always Cheapest

For generic drugs, the retail cash price is often dramatically higher than what's available through direct discount networks. Pharmacists are legally barred by gag clauses from disclosing lower prices.

Hidden Rebate Tiers

Manufacturers negotiate secret rebates with your insurer. Those savings are collected at the corporate level and never passed to you at the pharmacy counter.

No Single Source of Truth

GoodRx, Cost Plus, and retail pharmacies all quote different prices for the same drug. Vital Rx shows every source simultaneously — in one place, instantly — so the lowest price is obvious.

1. Who We Are

Vital Rx, Inc. ("Vital Rx," "we," "us," or "our") is a prescription price intelligence platform. We aggregate publicly available and partner-sourced drug pricing data and display it to consumers to help them make informed purchasing decisions. We are a data company — not a pharmacy, insurance company, or healthcare provider.

Our principal place of business is in the United States. For privacy questions, contact us at privacy@vitalrx.com.

2. What We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account data: Name, email address, and password when you create a Vital Rx account.
  • Profile data: Optional fields in your Vault — insurance information, date of birth, and saved medications. All Vault data is encrypted client-side before it is stored.
  • Search data: Medication names and dosages you search for. We use this to return accurate pricing results.
  • Support communications: If you contact support@vitalrx.com, we retain those messages to resolve your inquiry.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: Pages visited, features used, and session duration — used solely to improve the platform.
  • Device data: Browser type, operating system, and screen resolution for compatibility purposes.
  • IP address: Used for security, fraud prevention, and approximate geographic routing. Not linked to your health data.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect your prescription fill history from pharmacies.
We do not collect payment card or financial account data.
We do not collect your Social Security number or government ID.
We do not purchase or import third-party health records.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display accurate prescription pricing from our partner network
  • To generate and store your Digital Savings Card codes (BIN, PCN, Group)
  • To authenticate your account and maintain session security
  • To run the eligibility check that unlocks restricted manufacturer rates
  • To send you account-related notifications (never unsolicited marketing without opt-in)
  • To improve platform performance and fix technical issues
  • To comply with applicable law and respond to lawful requests

We do not use your health information to train machine learning models that are sold or licensed to third parties.

4. Who We Share With

We do not sell your personal information. Full stop.

We may share limited, anonymized or pseudonymized data with:

  • Pharmacy partner network: When you present your Digital Card at a pharmacy, your BIN/PCN/Group codes are transmitted to process the discount. Your name and account details are not shared.
  • Infrastructure providers: Secure cloud storage and authentication services (e.g., Firebase/Google Cloud) that process data on our behalf under strict data processing agreements.
  • Analytics services: Aggregated, non-identifiable usage statistics only. We do not share individual health search queries.
  • Law enforcement: Only when required by a valid court order or applicable law, and only to the extent legally required.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard safeguards to protect your information:

  • Client-side encryption: Sensitive Vault fields are encrypted in your browser before transmission and storage. Vital Rx staff cannot read your raw health data.
  • HTTPS everywhere: All traffic between your device and our servers is encrypted via TLS.
  • Minimal data retention: We retain only what is necessary. You can purge all your data from the Vault page at any time with one click.
  • Access controls: Internal access to user data is strictly limited and logged.

No system is perfectly secure. If you believe your account has been compromised, contact security@vitalrx.com immediately.

6. Cookies & Analytics

Vital Rx uses minimal cookies required for session management and authentication. We do not use third-party advertising trackers or behavioral profiling cookies.

We may use privacy-respecting analytics (such as aggregated page view counts) to understand how the platform is used. These analytics do not include personally identifiable information or individual health queries.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Update inaccurate information in your account or Vault.
  • Deletion: Use the "Purge All Data" function in your Vault, or email privacy@vitalrx.com. Deletion is permanent and irreversible.
  • Portability: Request your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Opt-out: Opt out of any marketing communications via the unsubscribe link in any email we send, or by contacting us directly.

California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA. Virginia, Colorado, and Connecticut residents may have rights under their respective state privacy laws. To exercise any right, contact privacy@vitalrx.com.

8. HIPAA Notice

Important: Vital Rx is a consumer price aggregation tool, not a covered entity or business associate as defined under HIPAA. We do not receive, transmit, or store Protected Health Information (PHI) as defined in 45 C.F.R. § 160.103 from covered healthcare providers.

While we design our data practices to align with HIPAA principles — encryption, minimal data collection, user control, and no commercial use of health data — our platform does not constitute HIPAA-covered services.

Federal law (42 U.S.C. § 1320a-7b) prohibits Medicare, Medicaid, VA, and TRICARE beneficiaries from using manufacturer savings cards when those costs are otherwise covered by a federal program. Vital Rx's eligibility check is designed to ensure compliance with this requirement.

9. Children's Privacy

Vital Rx is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, contact us at privacy@vitalrx.com and we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. For material changes, we will notify registered users via email. Your continued use of Vital Rx after a policy change constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Digital Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

Vital Rx is committed to making our platform accessible to everyone, including people with disabilities.

Our Commitment

Vital Rx strives to ensure its services are accessible to people with disabilities. We believe every person has the right to live with dignity, equality, comfort, and independence — and that includes accessing healthcare pricing information freely and easily.

Conformance Status

Vital Rx aims to conform to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible to people with disabilities, including those with visual, auditory, motor, and cognitive impairments.

Accessibility Features

  • Full keyboard navigation throughout the application
  • Screen-reader compatible — semantic HTML5 with ARIA roles and labels
  • Skip-to-content link for keyboard and screen-reader users
  • Sufficient color contrast ratios on all text and interactive elements
  • Visible focus indicators on all interactive elements
  • Reduced-motion support via prefers-reduced-motion media query
  • Descriptive alt text on all meaningful images
  • No content flashes more than three times per second
  • Responsive design that works across desktop, tablet, and mobile

Technical Specifications

Vital Rx relies on the following technologies for conformance with WCAG 2.1:

  • HTML5 with semantic landmark elements (header, main, nav, footer)
  • CSS3 with system-preference media queries
  • Vanilla JavaScript with accessible event handling and focus management

These pages are tested with modern browsers and assistive technologies including VoiceOver (macOS/iOS) and NVDA (Windows).

Known Limitations

Despite our best efforts, some limitations exist. We are actively working to resolve them:

  • Interactive price charts — SVG data visualizations may not be fully described for screen-reader users. Text-based alternatives are in progress.
  • Pharmacy location map — relies on browser geolocation APIs with varying assistive technology support across platforms.

Feedback & Contact

We welcome feedback on the accessibility of Vital Rx. If you encounter any barriers or have suggestions, please reach out — we aim to respond within 2 business days.

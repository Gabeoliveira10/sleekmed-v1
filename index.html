Clinical Transparency

Why You Overpay.

The prescription pricing system has three systemic failures. VITAL solves all three.

Co-Pays Aren't Always Cheapest

For generic drugs, your insurance co-pay is often higher than cash prices available through discount networks. Pharmacists are legally barred by gag clauses from disclosing this.

Hidden Rebate Tiers

Manufacturers negotiate secret rebates with your insurer. Those savings are collected at the corporate level and never passed to you at the pharmacy counter.

No Single Source of Truth

GoodRx, Cost Plus, and your insurance all quote different prices for the same drug. VITAL shows all five sources simultaneously — in one place, instantly.

Platform Education

How VITAL Works

A deep dive into the technology, network, and clinical logic behind our platform.

01

Our Pricing Engine

VITAL's pricing engine aggregates data from five sources: our proprietary affiliate network of 75,000+ pharmacies, the GoodRx pricing API, Mark Cuban's Cost Plus Drug Company, national insurance carrier formulary databases, and retail cash prices.

Note: Insurance co-pay estimates are based on formulary tier data and may vary by plan and deductible status.
02

The VITAL Direct Price

Our "VITAL Direct" price is the negotiated rate available through our proprietary affiliate card. When you present your Digital Access Card at a partner pharmacy, our BIN/PCN routing code is applied at the point of sale, and the negotiated price is honored immediately.

Affiliate Disclosure: VITAL earns a small affiliate fee per claim ($2.50–$5.00) from our partner network. This fee is never passed to the consumer.
03

Insurance Routing Logic

When you provide your insurance details in the Security Vault, VITAL cross-references your carrier and Group ID against our formulary database to calculate an estimated co-pay for your specific plan tier. This estimate is displayed alongside cash prices in every search result.

04

HIPAA & Data Security

All personal and insurance data stored in your Security Vault is encrypted client-side before storage. VITAL does not sell, share, or license your personal health information to any third party.

HIPAA Commitment: VITAL is designed to align with HIPAA data handling best practices for consumer health applications.

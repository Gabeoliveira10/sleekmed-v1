    Knowledge Hub

    Find answers and learn how to maximize your healthcare savings.

    What exactly is SleekMed?

    We are an unbiased healthcare price aggregator. We collect data from various discount networks, direct-to-consumer pharmacies, and standard insurers to show you all your options on one screen. We do not own pharmacies or sell drugs ourselves; we just provide the mathematical truth.

    How much does it cost to use?

    The SleekMed platform and the digital access card are 100% free to use. You simply pay the discounted price of the medication directly to the pharmacist at the counter. There are absolutely no hidden subscription fees, premium tiers, or forced upgrades.

    How do I use the digital access card?

    Create a free account and navigate to the 'Card' tab on the bottom menu. Show that specific screen to your pharmacist when dropping off or paying for your medication. They will enter the cryptographic routing numbers (BIN, PCN, Group) into their terminal to apply your discount instantly.

    Do I need insurance to use this platform?

    No insurance is required. In fact, many of our users find that our negotiated cash prices are actually significantly lower than their standard insurance co-pays, especially if they are on a high-deductible health plan (HDHP).

    Can I combine this with my insurance or Medicare?

    Our discounts cannot be legally stacked or combined simultaneously with insurance or Medicare on a single transaction. However, you can ask your pharmacist to "split-test" or compare our price against your insurance price, and you simply select whichever is cheaper for that specific transaction.

    Is my medical data secure?

    Absolutely. We utilize advanced enterprise-grade AES-256 encryption protocols. Your identity, insurance routing information, and medication history stored in your Personal Vault are secure. We make a strict pledge to never sell your data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

    About SleekMed

    Our mission to revolutionize healthcare pricing.

    The Problem We Are Solving

    For decades, the pharmaceutical supply chain has operated entirely in the shadows. Manufacturers sell to massive wholesalers, who sell to pharmacies, all while Pharmacy Benefit Managers (PBMs) negotiate secret rebates behind closed doors. The end result is that the patient at the counter has zero visibility into what a medication actually costs to produce and dispense.

    This severe lack of transparency leads to absurd situations where paying cash out-of-pocket is often cheaper than using a high-premium insurance plan. But because of corporate gag clauses and confusing adjudication systems, patients are rarely told this at the register.

    The SleekMed Solution

    SleekMed was born in Jacksonville, Florida with a simple premise: Healthcare data should be open, transparent, and accessible to everyone.

    We built a technology engine that aggregates pricing data from thousands of endpoints across the United States. We do not manufacture drugs and we do not own pharmacies. Our sole product is truth.

    By empowering patients with clear, comparative data before they reach the counter, we are forcing the industry to compete on price and efficiency rather than hidden, anti-competitive contracts.

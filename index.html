Why You Overpay for Prescriptions

01

Insurance Co-Pays Aren't Always Cheapest

For generic medications, your insurance co-pay is often higher than the cash price available through discount networks. Pharmacies are forbidden by gag clauses from telling you this without being asked.

02

Price Opacity by Design

Drug manufacturers set one list price but negotiate dozens of secret rebate tiers. The price you see at the counter is the price after your insurer's rebate was already collected.

03

No Single Source of Truth

GoodRx, Cost Plus, and your insurance each have different price points for the same drug. SleekMed shows all prices in one place, instantly.
